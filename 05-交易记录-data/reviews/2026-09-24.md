## Hypotheses

Twenty-five decisions, zero closed trades. Everything here is a hypothesis; the five-decision bar is met and
the trade bar is not.

- *(n=25 decisions; 12 constructions priced in one check, 0 overlapping)* **The span screen and the 1.5 net
  reward/risk floor are not merely hard to satisfy together on this structure — they are disjoint.** At 22:00
  the four constructions that passed the span screen paid **0.516, 0.786, 0.908 and 1.027, every one below
  the floor**, and the one construction that cleared the floor (3.781) rested on a stop at **0.26x N**. The
  two conditions did not overlap anywhere in the set. This is what L-004 has been asserting from repeated
  near-misses; it is the first time the whole construction space has been laid out and shown not to
  intersect. **It is also the reason the day's three narrow misses (0.020pp, 0.003pp, 0.077pp) are not
  progress**: a screen you nearly pass while the floor you would then fail sits on the other side of the same
  trade-off is not a screen you are close to clearing. → the strongest evidence **P-006** has.
- *(n=25 decisions; 4 instances)* **The evidence gate has never been the binding constraint, and the checks
  that cleared it died on geometry and book depth exactly like the ones that did not.** Instances: 09-23

### Call 1 — ETHUSDT is caught by L-001's higher-frame amendment. The tradable set is empty.

`LESSONS.md` currently names 2850.00 as an ETHUSDT artifact print and says both ETH prints have "since aged
out". **That judgement was made against the 1h window, before the amendment requiring the 4h and daily

**It is excluded anyway, for four reasons.** (1) The experiment's own earlier judgement already classified

caught reading 2.2x at 1h and 33.9x at 4h. (3) L-001's re-test condition is explicit: no contamination of any
24h, 4h **or daily** field. ETH fails it. (4) Ruling the other way would be the one thing this review is

tradable. **An empty tradable set is a correct outcome of the rules as written, not a problem for the review
to solve.** The 21:00 check said so itself, against its own interest, and it was right.

**Consequence, stated plainly: as of this review all five watchlist contracts are excluded under L-001, and

## Decisions reviewed

Five decisions, all `no_trade`. All five were sound on what was knowable at the time. What makes them worth
reading is that the reason for refusing changed across the window, and the last one was not a judgement at
all.

**`check_2200` (09-21, 22:20 MYT, +20 min — not late) — `no_trade`. Correct.** The 21:00 note had named
exactly what would make the XRPUSDT long tradeable: a break *and hold* above 1.4969 on expanding volume, or
a retest of 1.4847/1.4894 that holds. Both were tested and both failed — price broke the 1.4847 range floor
instead, and the largest 5m bar was the down bar. Price action and volume, which had been absent and
contrary respectively, now both argued against the long. ETH's pullback printed 1.40 net, still below the
floor. A pre-named condition being tested and failing, with the trade dropped rather than re-argued at a
new price, is the behaviour the journal chain exists to produce.

**`check_2300` (09-21, 23:14 MYT, +14 min — not late) — `no_trade`. Correct, and the most tempting refusal
so far.** XRP reclaimed 1.4894 and made a new high — the trigger that had been missing. It was still
declined, because the named volume condition failed (the heaviest 5m bar was the 14:55Z down bar, hourly
volume contracting, the breakout fading eight minutes in) and because the 1.5270 target now sat closer than
one hourly range, so every structurally honest stop printed 0.70–1.50 net. The decision records that XRP
*momentarily printed exactly 1.50 net at 15:13:50Z* and states explicitly that it was declined on the
evidence and the geometry rather than on the window. Declining a setup at the exact moment its headline
number touches the floor, and saying so in the file, is the hardest version of this discipline.

**`check_0000` (09-22, 00:19 MYT, +19 min — not late) — `no_trade`. Correct.** The 23:00 note had set a
three-part confirmation test in advance (hold above 1.4969 for 45+ minutes, hourly volume above a named
threshold, no heavier down bar). All three failed: price was back to 1.4953, the 15Z hour contracted, and
the heaviest 5m bar was the 15:15Z down bar that erased the breakout. The long's ratio *passed* at the
decision-time quote — 2.343 and 3.107 net on two tighter stops — and was refused because those stops were
invalid and the pre-named test had failed, which the decision states explicitly. The failed breakout's
mirror-image short was then also refused, at 0.543–1.452 net on every honest stop above the 1.5077 high.
Both directions held to the same standard, again.

**`check_0200` (09-22, 02:17 MYT, +17 min — not late) — `no_trade`. Correct, and the most revealing.**

## What worked / what did not

**Worked**

- **Pre-naming the condition, then honouring the answer.** Every check in this window was set up by the
  previous one with explicit thresholds, and each one tested them and reported failure rather than
  re-deriving a reason to trade. The 23:00 and 00:00 checks are the clearest: both refused XRP after its
  trigger fired, because the *named* volume condition did not.
- **Two quotes minutes apart, every check.** This is the single most productive habit the experiment has
  developed. It turned 2.122 into 1.273, 1.591 into 0.747, and exposed an XRP short whose ratio improved
  only because price drifted onto its stop. A ratio computed from one quote is not a measurement.
- **Refusing noise-width stops.** The 09:00 check found an XRPUSDT long at **4.615 net on a 0.060% stop**
  against a 0.883% maximum 5m bar range — a stop one fifteenth of a single bar — and BNBUSDT passing in
  **both directions simultaneously** on mirror stops 0.155% and 0.166% away. A contract clearing the floor
  both ways at once is proof the geometry is producing the number, and all three were refused.
- **The late-run rule fired correctly on its second day and cost nothing.**
- **Running the artifact scan at 1h as well as 5m**, which found two BTC artifacts the 5m scan missed.
- **News read as evidence against the position being considered**, not for it: the 09-21 rally was reported
  as a short squeeze that liquidated more than USD 648m of BTC shorts above 85,000, and that was recorded
  as a reason not to sell the pullback. Given where XRP finished, it was the most useful item on the board.

**Did not work**

- Host reliability: two stalls in two days, four slots lost, cause still unidentified.
- BTCUSDT is effectively not in the experiment — six consecutive checks excluded, now on spread as well as
  artifacts. The watchlist is five contracts on paper and four in practice.
- Two days, 240 planned checks pro-rated to 16, eight decisions, **zero trades**. Not a failure in itself —
  every individual refusal was correct — but it is now the central fact about the experiment.

## Hypotheses

Eight decisions, zero closed trades. Everything here remains a hypothesis; the sample-size bar for a lesson
is met on decisions (8 ≥ 5), so three items below have been promoted to `LESSONS.md` as hypotheses.

- *(n=8 decisions, 5 separate constructions)* **The 1.5 net reward/risk floor and the structural-stop
  requirement are jointly selecting against this watchlist at these volatilities — and this is now the
  central structural finding of the experiment.** It has appeared in three different forms which are one
  problem: (a) the 00:00 finding that XRP produces nothing valid until an honest stop and target sit more
  than roughly 2.5% apart; (b) the 02:00 finding that a target only 1.6% away against ~1.3% hourly ranges
  makes the confirmation wait unaffordable — by the time the setup is confirmed, the target is gone; (c) the
  09:00 finding that on the two contracts with genuine directional structure the *nearest honest* target
  paid 0.194–0.884 net, and only a target beyond two or three intervening supports cleared the floor. When
  the honest target is too close to pay and the paying target is unreachable, the filter is not selecting
  good trades from bad — it is selecting nothing. → Proposal P-006, and note carefully: **the review cannot
  and does not propose lowering the floor.** A filter that rejects everything is a finding to hand to
  Edward, not a rule to relax.
- *(n=8 decisions, 6 checks)* **Demo artifacts are a BTCUSDT phenomenon, not a host-wide one.** Six
  contaminated BTC prints against two on ETH, one on SOL, none on BNB or XRP — plus a 32.59 bps spread
  breach that resolved to 1.06 bps in three minutes. Promoted to a lesson.
- *(n=8 decisions)* **A reward/risk that clears the floor on a stop narrower than recent bar noise is
  geometry, not edge.** Promoted to a lesson.
- *(n=8 decisions, 4 constructions)* **A ratio that improves because price moved toward the stop is not an
  improving trade.** Promoted to a lesson.
- *(n=4 decisions)* **A target that clears the floor only by jumping intervening support or resistance is
  not a valid target** — XRP long reaching 1.5441 (00:00), XRP short reaching 1.4849 past 1.5041 and 1.4973
  (09:00), SOL failing in both directions (09:00), the 1.5270 target closer than one hourly range (23:00).
  Held back from `LESSONS.md`: four decisions support it, one short of the five-decision bar. Promote it if
  a fifth appears.
- *(n=2 days, 4 lost slots)* **Scheduled execution on this host is degrading, not stabilising.** One slot
  lost on day 1, three on day 2.

## Lessons

**Three lessons added; none removed.** The bar is 3 trades or 5 decisions; the experiment now has **8
recorded decisions**, so the decision bar is met. All three are phrased as hypotheses, as required with
fewer than 30 closed trades, and all three make future decisions strictly more selective:

1. **BTCUSDT is structurally unreadable on this host** — decline entries while artifacts keep printing, and
   run the artifact scan at 1h as well as 5m. *(6 BTC prints vs 3 on all other contracts combined, 8
   decisions.)*
2. **Require the structural stop to be wider than recent bar noise.** *(8 decisions; XRP long 4.615 net on
   a 0.060% stop against a 0.883% bar range; BNB passing both directions at once on 0.155%/0.166%.)*
3. **Requote before acting on any ratio; a ratio that improves because price drifted toward the stop is not
   an improving trade.** *(8 decisions, 4 constructions: ETH 1.34→0.55, SOL 2.122→1.273, BNB 1.591→0.747,
   XRP short 1.736→2.809 by drift alone.)*

None of these permits anything that was previously forbidden.

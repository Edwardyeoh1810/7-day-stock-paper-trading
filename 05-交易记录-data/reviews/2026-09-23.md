meant day 3 was never reviewed. → P-007 amended with day-3 evidence.

**2. The cap-breach count is inflated by one and the error propagated between checks.** Detailed above. A
check asserted a breach that its own predecessor's file does not contain, and the next check inherited the
running total without re-deriving it. Low severity in itself; it matters because running totals carried
forward in prose are exactly how a small error becomes a "finding". → P-008.

**3. A timestamp was estimated rather than measured — and was then caught and corrected by the check
itself.** `2026-09-23_check_2100.json` carries an explicit `timestamp_correction` block recording that
`checked_at_local` originally read 21:32+08:00, "an ESTIMATE written without reading the clock, not a
measured time", against a true 21:11 and an engine consumption at 13:13:18Z. It also records that the
engine's deterministic export embeds the wrong value and **was not edited**. The error is clerical and
changes nothing material — every measured timestamp in the file is independently sourced and the run was
on time either way. It is recorded here as a **correctly handled** error: disclosed at the top of the file,
scoped honestly, and not papered over by editing a generated artifact.

**4. Slot coverage is still miscounted at the review boundary — and this review watched the miscount
resolve.** The first pass of this review read `2026-09-23_check_2200` as missed one minute after it came
due; the decision landed thirteen minutes later and the regenerated `stats.json` moved it into the decided
column. **The counter was wrong for fourteen minutes and would have been read as a lost slot by anyone who
only saw the 22:01 figure.** Third consecutive review to make the same correction by hand, and the first to
have the correction confirmed inside the same session. Low severity, entirely cosmetic so far, and it will
stop being cosmetic the day a review is read by anyone who does not know to apply the grace window. P-005
is open and its evidence is now as strong as it needs to be.

**5. A review that closed its window early, corrected in the same session (low severity, disclosed).** The
first pass deferred the 22:00 check to tomorrow's review. That was defensible at 22:06 — the decision did
not yet exist — but it would have meant the most consequential check of the day was reviewed a day late and
against a note whose prediction had already resolved. The window was reopened and the check folded in. The
general rule this suggests: **a review that runs at the same time as a check should wait for that check
rather than close its window in front of it**, because the checks are 45 minutes wide by rule and the review
is not racing anything. Noted here rather than proposed as a schedule change, since the review may not
change the schedule; if it recurs, it becomes a proposal.

**6. No engine rejections, no rule violations, no backfilling, no lesson loosened by a check.** Every
decision in the window carried a timing check, a spread check, a funding check, an artifact-exclusion list
and an explicit "lessons applied" section. Four separate checks recorded that a lesson's re-test condition
had been met or exceeded and declined to act on it, correctly deferring to this review — the 22:00 check
being the clearest, recording BTC's cleanest-ever scan and explicitly writing that "whether the clean window
starts the re-test clock is the review's call, not this check's." Nothing under `reviews/` was touched by
any check, and the 22:00 decision explicitly records that it did not read the folder for modification
because the review task owns it and might be running concurrently.

## What worked / what did not

**Worked**

- **Prospective falsifiable conditions, written by one check and resolved by the market at the next — four
  times in the window, and twice resolved *against* trading.** 16:00 predicted that a further advance would
  make the XRP long worse rather than better, and it did. 16:00 named 1.6043 as the level whose loss would
  end the ascending sequence and hand the short a trend leg; it broke, and 20:00 recorded it. 20:00 set a
  condition for claiming `market_context` on the XRP short (the 24h figure must turn negative while 4h/8h
  weakness persists); at 21:00 the weakness persisted and the 24h figure did not turn, so the condition
  declined to fire — **and at 22:00 it fired, and the short was priced honestly and refused anyway**, which
  is the outcome that proves the condition was a test rather than a trigger. As the 21:00 note puts it: a
  condition that only ever fires in favour of trading is not a test. The 21:00 note also set the BNB
  condition (a genuine lower high between 783.46 and 786.19); at 22:00 no such high existed and the trade
  stayed refused.
- **A check predicting its successor's arithmetic trap, to two thousandths of a percent — the strongest
  single piece of process evidence the experiment has produced.** 21:00 wrote that by 22:00 the 12:00Z
  breakdown bar would roll out of the window and BNB's N would collapse toward **0.222%**, mechanically
  flattering the same stop on the same structure, and told its successor not to let the passage of time
  manufacture a pass. Measured at 22:00: **0.224%**. The flattered stop then scored best on every number at
  the check and was refused. This is the difference between a lesson that is written down and a lesson that
  is operating.
- **Refusing to claim a category that had turned favourable.** Four separate instances, which together are
  the strongest discipline the experiment has shown. Funding at 16:00, declined *because* it had flipped to
  favour the long after alternating four times in seven settlements. `market_context` at 20:00, declined for
  the short because the same field had been claimed for the long on a different window four hours earlier.
  Volume at 21:00, declined although BNB's breakdown bar was genuinely the largest of 26, because one candle
  is not participation and the 1h frame disagreed. **And a fourth at 22:00, the strongest of them:
  `market_context` for the XRP short, whose enabling condition had just fired**, declined because XRP was
  simultaneously the *strongest* of the five on the very 24h window that crossed — and because the crossing
  was partly an excluded artifact leaving the window. Volume was declined a second time at 22:00 on a sharper
  ground: BNB's largest recent 5m bar was a **bounce** bar, so claiming volume for the short would have
  required reading it backwards.
- **The span screen, invented at 00:00 and applied prospectively at every check since.** It has disqualified
  the check's own best candidate six times running, in both directions, and it is the only thing that stood
  between this experiment and a 1.724 entry at 02:00 that L-002 would have waved through. Its cleanest
  statement came at 22:00, on ETHUSDT: the book was the best of the five and improving, the evidence was
  arguable, and the structure simply did not offer the span — 1.474 to the nearest usable target, with the
  next real structure 2.53% away.
- **Measuring N over the full preceding hour rather than the last twelve bars.** At 21:00 this single
  measurement choice turned BNB's only passing stop from 2.89x N into 0.80x N and killed the trade; at 22:00
  it did the same work in reverse, holding the same stop at **0.49x N** against the window containing the
  setup bar while the twelve-bar window flattered it to 1.76x N. One measurement choice, two checks, the
  same trade refused for the same real reason.
- **Sampling the book three times instead of twice**, on the 20:00 note's own instruction that a single
  healthy sample means nothing on this host. It is what caught BNB's 3,798x collapse-and-recovery and ETH's
  bid failing to return — and at 22:00 a **7,497x** BNB collapse, XRP's bid at 55 USDT, and BTC's book going
  on both sides. Six consecutive checks; the instability has now hit every one of the five contracts.
- **Day 3 lost no slots, and nine consecutive checks ran on time.**

**Did not work**

- **The review task itself stalled for nearly a full day.** Three stalls in three days; the cause remains
  unidentified.
- **The demo's price level drifts several percent from the outside world, and the sign flips.** At 16:00 the
  demo was ~2.1% *above* external XRP prints; at 20:00 it was ~2.9% *below*. Direction tracked reality both
  times. A host whose level wanders ±3% while its direction is right is one on which no stop or target
  within a few percent of a demo print can be trusted.
- **Predicted funding was wrong by a full sign two hours before settlement** (+0.006982% forecast against a
  −0.009057% print). The forward rate is not a usable read on this host.
- **The demo's volume series carries no directional information.** Nine consecutive checks examined it and
  claimed it zero times. XRPUSDT's 1h band stayed flat through a new 24h high *and* through a 7% reversal —
  both on below-median volume. At 22:00 the only expansion on the board pointed the wrong way: BNB's largest
  recent 5m bar was the bounce bar against the short. That is a finding about the host, not about XRP.
- **The watchlist is five contracts on paper and two in practice, having got worse today.** BTCUSDT excluded
  for fifteen consecutive checks; SOLUSDT excluded for five; and **XRPUSDT now carries its own artifact**,
  which under L-001's rule bites on any contract with an unrevised print in the current window. On the 22:00
  evidence only ETHUSDT and BNBUSDT are cleanly readable, and both of those failed on geometry and book. The
  correction recorded at 22:00 also matters here: SOLUSDT's cap breach was **transient**, so the exclusion
  rests on the artifact, not the spread.
- **A signal the experiment had been waiting for turned out to be a measurement artifact.** XRP's 24h
  crossing zero was the condition 20:00 set, 21:00 tested and 22:00 saw fire — and it fired partly because an
  excluded spike left the comparison window. Two of the three "developments" of the last two checks were
  windows moving rather than markets moving.
- **Three days, seventeen decisions, zero trades.** Every individual refusal was correct. It remains the
  central fact about the experiment.

## Hypotheses

Seventeen decisions, zero closed trades. Everything here is a hypothesis; the five-decision bar is met on
decisions and the trade bar is not.

- *(n=17 decisions, 3 instances across 2 consecutive checks)* **A change produced by the measurement window
  moving is not a market event, and on this host it is the most persuasive kind of false signal — because it
  arrives exactly when the number finally crosses the threshold you were waiting for.** Three instances, all
  in the last two checks. **(1)** BNB's noise floor fell from 0.800% to 0.224% between 21:00 and 22:00 for no
  reason except that the setup-defining bar aged out of the twelve-bar window, making an unchanged stop on
  unchanged structure look 2.2x safer. **(2)** XRP's 24h relative figure crossed from +0.886% to −0.139%
  partly because the excluded 1.6855 spike decayed out of the 24h comparison, firing the very condition the
  20:00 note had set as the gate for the short. **(3)** BTCUSDT's artifact scan came back clean at 09:00 and
  16:00 and dirty again at 20:00, as prints aged out of and reappeared in the 30-bar window, while the
  contamination that actually matters — the 09-21 prints in the 4h and daily frames — never moved at all. In
  every case the honest test is the same: **re-measure against a window that contains the bar or the event
  that defines the setup, and ask whether anything entered or left the window between checks.** → promoted
  to **L-006**.
- *(n=17 decisions, 6 consecutive checks)* **A valid entry needs the clean structural span from stop to
  *nearest* structural target to be at least ~2.5N + ~0.14% drag, where N is the largest 5m bar range of the
  preceding hour — and on this watchlist it usually is not.** Escalating evidence of what "passing" costs:
  at 02:00 the passing target sat beyond four rejected highs; at 09:00 beyond the end of the data; at 16:00
  on a print the outside world does not confirm; at 20:00 below the 24h low in a vacuum; at 21:00 on ETH at
  2670, "a price with no structure of any kind"; and at 22:00 on ETH again, refused on the span alone with
  the best book of the five. → promoted to **L-004**.
- *(n=17 decisions, 6 consecutive checks, all 5 contracts now affected)* **Top-of-book *quantity* on this demo
  host is unstable by orders of magnitude within minutes, and the 25 bps spread cap cannot see it.** ETH bid
  82 / ask 16 USDT while quoting 0.15 bps; XRP bid 22,146,602 → 5 USDT; BNB bid 36,721,037 → 9,669 →
  20,187,299 in 217 seconds; ETH bid 245,634 → 111 → 217; and at 22:00 BNB 8,606,494 → **1,148** → 2,782,512
  (7,497x in 124 seconds), XRP to **55 USDT**, and BTC gone on *both* sides on the third sample. The
  oscillation is the finding, not any single print: a reduce-only stop resting on this book between checks
  cannot be assumed to fill near its trigger. → carried by **L-005**, and → P-009 for the rule change this
  review cannot make.
- *(n=17 decisions, 5 contaminated contracts — now including XRPUSDT)* **Demo artifacts are host-wide, not a
  BTCUSDT phenomenon, and the artifact test is frame-dependent.** The largest print of the experiment (SOL
  106.67, ~570x) is not on BTC; the book instability has now hit all five contracts; and XRP's 1.6855 spike
  read as *unremarkable* on the 1h frame (2.2x) and as an artifact on the 4h and daily frames (34x), which
  are the frames swing levels are drawn on. → **L-001 amended** on both points; the scope claim is withdrawn
  and the scan is extended past 1h.
- *(n=17 decisions, 9 consecutive checks)* **The demo's volume series carries no directional information on
  this watchlist.** Claimed zero times in nine checks; a new 24h high and a 7% reversal both on below-median
  1h volume. Not yet a lesson: it currently functions as a permanent absence rather than a rule that changes
  a decision, and making it a lesson would just restate the two-category bar. Watch for a check where volume
  *would* have been the second category.
- *(n=9 funding settlements, 4 sign alternations)* **XRPUSDT funding on this host oscillates and its forward
  rate is unreliable**, having been wrong by a full sign two hours before settlement. `derivatives` should be
  treated as degraded to nothing usable on this host, not merely to "funding alone". Held back from
  LESSONS.md: it is a statement about a data feed rather than a decision rule, and P-004 already carries it.
- *(n=3 days, 3 stalls)* **Scheduled execution on this host stalls roughly once a day, and the trading
  checks recovered while the review did not.** Day 3 lost no check slots but lost the review.
- *(n=2 consecutive checks)* **The binding constraint has moved from evidence to geometry and execution.**
  At 21:00, two contracts cleared the two-category bar honestly and failed on stop placement, target
  structure and book depth. At 22:00 it happened again and harder: BNB held a claimable `trend` *and*
  `market_context`, XRP's second category came within one contested field of firing, and all of them died on
  a sub-noise stop, a vacuum target and an unquoted book. Two checks is still not a pattern, but it is no
  longer one observation. **This remains the single most informative thing to watch over days 4–7, because
  it separates "the AI will not trade" from "the filter and the host will not let it"** — and if days 4–7
  keep producing cleared evidence bars that die on execution, the honest conclusion is about the host, not
  about the strategy.

## Lessons

**Three lessons added (L-004, L-005, L-006), two amended (L-001, L-002), none removed. Six lessons total,
against a cap of ten.** The bar is 3 trades or 5 decisions; the experiment has **17 recorded decisions**, so
the decision bar is met and the trade bar is not. All are phrased as hypotheses, as required with fewer than
30 closed trades, and all make future decisions **strictly more selective**. No lesson permits anything
previously forbidden.

Two judgements worth stating plainly.

**(1) L-001's re-test condition for BTCUSDT was met at 09:00, exceeded at 16:00, lost at 20:00 and produced
the cleanest scan of the experiment at 22:00 — and BTCUSDT is still not being un-excluded.** The 22:00 scan
found no new BTC print at either resolution across a 30-hour 1h window and a 2.5-hour 5m window. That is
real progress and it is recorded as such. It is not the condition, which the amendment states as a full day
clean **and** no contamination of any 24h or daily field: the 09-21 prints still sit in the 4h and daily
frames that any level would be drawn on. Beyond that, the lesson's underlying scope claim was falsified in
this same window, which argues for widening the exclusion rather than narrowing it; and retiring an
exclusion is a loosening, which this review may not do on three days of data and zero trades. **The re-test
clock starts at one clean check, not zero — that is a factual record, not a relaxation**, and the exclusion
binds in full until a full clean day including the 4h and daily frames.

**(2) XRPUSDT now falls inside L-001's rule, and the rule is extended past 1h.** The lesson's operative
sentence already excludes "any contract carrying an unrevised artifact print in the current window", so
XRP's 1.6855 spike brings it in without the list of named contracts having to be exhaustive — the names are
illustrative and the rule is the rule. What is genuinely new is that the print reads clean at 1h and dirty
at 4h and daily, so the scan is extended to the 4h and daily frames. Both changes make the AI more
selective.

1. **L-001 (amended twice) — treat any contract carrying an unrevised artifact as structurally unreadable;
   artifacts are host-wide and the scan runs at 5m, 1h *and* the 4h/daily frames.** Scope claim withdrawn;
   XRPUSDT now caught by the rule. *(17 decisions; SOL 106.67 at ~570x persisting five checks; BTC excluded
   15 consecutive checks; XRP 1.6855 reading 2.2x at 1h and 34x at 4h.)*
2. **L-002 (amended) — a stop narrower than recent bar noise is a position size, not a stop — and N is
   measured over the full preceding hour, not a convenient recent window.** *(17 decisions; decisive in six
   consecutive checks; at 21:00 the window choice moved BNB's stop from 2.89x N to 0.80x N and at 22:00 from
   1.76x N to 0.49x N on the identical stop and structure.)*
3. **L-003 (unchanged) — requote before acting on any ratio.** Both directions demonstrated, and at 22:00 in
   a new and stronger form: the drift **rescued a construction that had failed on arithmetic an hour
   earlier**, carrying it 1.210 → 2.033 through the floor at the exact moment the premise broke. Re-test
   clock stays at zero. *(17 decisions, 8 consecutive checks where the requote was material.)*
4. **L-004 (new) — run the span screen before pricing any ratio, and price the nearest structural target.**
   *(17 decisions, 6 consecutive checks in which it disqualified the check's own best candidate; at 22:00
   ETHUSDT's nearest honest target paid 1.474 with the next structure 2.53% away.)*
5. **L-005 (new) — sample top-of-book notional at least three times and refuse the trade if the side the
   trade must cross goes effectively unquoted.** *(17 decisions, 6 consecutive checks; all five contracts now
   affected, BNB collapsing 7,497x in 124 seconds at 22:00.)*
6. **L-006 (new) — before treating any threshold crossing as a market event, check whether the measurement
   window moved.** Re-measure against a window that contains the setup-defining bar, and ask what entered or
   left the window since the last check. *(17 decisions, 3 instances across 2 consecutive checks: BNB's N
   collapsing 0.800% → 0.224% on an unchanged stop; XRP's 24h crossing zero as an excluded artifact decayed
   out; BTC's scan reading clean then dirty then clean as prints aged in and out while the 4h contamination
   never moved.)* This is strictly a new reason to refuse and cannot permit anything.

## Proposals

Open and awaiting Edward: **P-001**, **P-002**, **P-003**, **P-004** (derivatives degraded — evidence
amended again today: the forward funding rate was wrong by a full sign two hours out, and the
forecast-versus-outcome test has now been deferred twice because settlement keeps falling outside the check
window), **P-005** (evidence now conclusive: the 22:00 slot was reported missed at 22:01 and decided at
22:14, inside this review's own session), **P-006** (evidence strengthened: nine more decisions, and two
consecutive checks in which the evidence bar was cleared and geometry and execution refused the trade —
which is the real test of whether P-006 describes the filter or the market), **P-007** (amended: a third
stall, this time on the review task itself), and three new:

- **P-008** — require running totals asserted in a decision file to be re-derived from the underlying files
  rather than inherited from the previous check's prose. Raised because the cap-breach count reached "five"
  through a breach that no file records. Evidence strengthened today: the 22:00 check recorded **two**
  findings against its own predecessor's (SOL's cap breach was transient, not monotonic; BTC had the worst
  book of the five having been called the only stable one an hour earlier), which is the behaviour this
  proposal wants and the reason the inherited-prose habit is worth closing off.
- **P-009** — add a top-of-book notional floor to the pre-trade checks, sampled at least three times, and
  treat the exit side of the intended position as the binding side. The 25 bps spread cap is blind to
  quantity: the tightest spread of the experiment, 0.15 bps on ETHUSDT, sat on a **16 USDT** ask. This is a
  rule and code change and is therefore Edward's alone; L-005 makes the AI refuse such books in the
  meantime, but only the rule can make the engine refuse them. Evidence strengthened today: at 22:00 **not
  one of the five contracts** held a quoted entry-side book across three samples taken inside 170 seconds.
- **P-010 (new)** — have the tooling flag measurement-window transitions, so a check can see when a number
  moved because a bar left the window rather than because the market moved. Raised because **both** headline
  developments of the 22:00 check were window artifacts, and one of them fired a condition the experiment
  had spent two checks waiting for. L-006 makes the AI check for this by hand; the proposal is to make it
  visible automatically, which is a tooling change and therefore Edward's.

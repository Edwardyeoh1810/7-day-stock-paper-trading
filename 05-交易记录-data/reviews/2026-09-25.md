  2682.25. At 00:00 → 02:00 → 09:00 the 16:00Z 4h bar read 2676.00, then 2662.20, then closed at 2694.09.
  That is three different answers inside one bar. Each check that refused the forming read was right to.
- **Stating the binding N before the rolled-forward N** (09:00). This was the first case where the order of
  measurement decided the answer. See Hypotheses.
- **Isolation tests on every big move** (20:00, 21:00, 22:00). Each move was treated as real because it was
  broad, which cut against the artifact narrative.
- **Refusing false `market_context` and `derivatives` signals** (00:00): the zero crossings came from the 24h
  window rolling, and funding unclamped by a hair.

**Did not work.**
- Scan depth and record quality degraded once the answer was known in advance (Process errors 2 and 3). An
  empty tradable set makes every check look redundant. The checks still need to produce re-test data, and at
  the moment they are not.
- Point forecasts of N failed three times today, all in the tightening direction (see L-007).

## Hypotheses

Thirty-three decisions and zero closed trades, so everything here is a hypothesis.

- *(n=5 consecutive ETH checks, 09-24 23:00 → 09-25 16:00)* **The evidence gate is unstable at hourly
  resolution, and its "independent" categories move together.** The gate read **3 short → 0 → 1 short → 1 long
  → 0**. The single surviving category switched sides between 02:00 and 09:00. At 23:00, `trend`,
  `price_action` and `volume` were all read off the same handful of bars, so one closing bar (12:00Z 4h)
  removed two of them at once. If this holds, a gate that passes at one check is weaker evidence than two
  categories suggest. **Not yet a lesson:** the gate has passed only once on today's contract, so there is
  nothing yet to show that persistence adds value. With the set empty it cannot change a decision anyway.
  Watch it.
- *(n=33 decisions; 10 written forecasts, 7 confirmed, 3 missed, all 3 misses in the tightening direction)*
  **Rolling windows only ever surprise on the loose side when nothing prints; the market printing is what
  breaks a forecast, and it always breaks it toward tighter.** The 09:00 check showed the stakes: against the
  binding window the span screen fails by 0.071pp; against the rolled-forward window it passes by 0.154pp.
  Measurement order alone would have produced an entry. L-007 has been updated with this record.
- *(n=5 of 5 contracts, 5 daily bars)* **Under L-001 as written, the experiment is over as a trading test.**
  Every exclusion sits in a daily bar that outlives 2026-10-20 in a 30-bar window. This is arithmetic, not a
  judgement, and only Edward can change it (P-011).
- *(n=8 checks today)* **L-005 remains the most consistent independent refusal.** ETH ask 500, 29.64, 26.95,
  5.35 and 27 USDT at different checks. ETH went unquoted on both sides at 16:00, and at two of three sweeps at
  21:00. The 25 bps cap fired at **two** checks today (XRP 54.38 bps at 21:00, 27.79–42.91 bps at all three
  sweeps at 22:00), after 16 consecutive blind checks. Both breaches were on XRP, which is already excluded.
- *(n=3 instances carried from day 4, 0 new claims)* **Volume as a contrary signal.** It was claimed once at
  23:00 (supporting the short), refused the long at 00:00, and dissolved on re-measurement at 02:00. That is
  consistent with day 4's hypothesis but adds no clean instance. Still not a lesson.
